# RentSaathi — Product Context

## What is RentSaathi?

RentSaathi is a **trust-first rental property marketplace** built specifically for Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian cities. Unlike generic platforms like 99acres or MagicBricks that cater primarily to metros, RentSaathi focuses on the unique rental dynamics of smaller cities — where trust, local networks, and WhatsApp-based communication are the norm.

**Launch City:** Alwar, Rajasthan

---

## The Problem

In Tier-2/Tier-3 Indian cities like Alwar:

1. **No dedicated rental platform** — Most listings are scattered across local WhatsApp groups, Facebook Marketplace, or word-of-mouth
2. **Trust deficit** — Tenants don't know if a listing is from a genuine owner or a fraudulent broker
3. **Information asymmetry** — Rent prices, availability, and property conditions are hard to compare
4. **Communication friction** — Tenants and owners prefer WhatsApp over phone calls or in-app chats
5. **Broker dominance** — Many tenants pay unnecessary brokerage because they can't find direct owner listings

---

## The Solution

RentSaathi provides:

- **Centralized listing platform** — All rental properties in one searchable place
- **Trust system** — Verified/Unverified badges so tenants can make informed decisions
- **Admin-curated quality** — Every listing is reviewed before going live (no spam/fake listings)
- **WhatsApp-first contact** — One tap to message the owner/broker directly on WhatsApp
- **Transparency** — Clear "Listed by Owner" vs "Listed by Broker" tags

---

## Target Users

### Tenants (Primary)
- Young professionals, students, small business owners in Alwar
- Looking for 1BHK/2BHK/PG/Shop rentals
- Want quick, trustworthy information without physical visits to brokers
- Prefer WhatsApp communication

### Property Owners (Supply Side)
- Individual homeowners with 1-3 rental properties
- Want to find tenants without paying broker commissions
- Not tech-savvy — need a simple listing process

### Brokers (Supply Side)
- Local property brokers who manage multiple listings
- Want a digital presence to reach more tenants
- Willing to list properties transparently

### Admin (Internal)
- RentSaathi team member who reviews and approves listings
- Ensures quality and prevents spam/fake entries

---

## Core Value Proposition

> "Find verified rental properties in Alwar. Contact owners directly on WhatsApp. No spam. No fake listings."

### For Tenants:
- Browse real, admin-approved listings
- Trust badges help identify genuine properties
- One-tap WhatsApp contact — no account needed to browse

### For Owners/Brokers:
- Free listing platform (MVP — no charges)
- Reach tenants actively searching for rentals
- Simple form to list a property in under 5 minutes

---

## Business Model (Post-MVP)

The MVP is completely free. Future monetization options include:
- **Featured listings** — Pay to appear at the top of search results
- **Verified badge for brokers** — Monthly subscription for professional brokers
- **Premium listing slots** — Limited spots on homepage featured section

---

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is earned** — Every listing is reviewed. Verified badges mean something.
2. **Local first** — Built for Alwar's rental market dynamics, not generic.
3. **WhatsApp is king** — Don't fight user behavior. Enable it.
4. **Simple over clever** — No complex flows. List in 5 minutes. Browse in 30 seconds.
5. **Mobile-first** — 80%+ users will access on smartphones.

---

## What RentSaathi is NOT (MVP boundaries)

- ❌ Not a payments platform (no rent collection)
- ❌ Not a chat app (WhatsApp handles communication)
- ❌ Not a legal service (no rent agreements)
- ❌ Not a national platform (Alwar only for now)
- ❌ Not a subscription service (free for MVP)
- ❌ Not a mobile app (web-only, responsive)
